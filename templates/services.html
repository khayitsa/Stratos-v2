The work involved in establishing a UAE business does not end when the licence is issued. Stratos connects the structure, formation, banking, residence, bookkeeping and administrative workstreams required to move from an initial plan to a functioning company.

One Business. Connected Decisions.

The proposed activities affect the licence. The ownership affects the documents required. The operating model affects the choice of jurisdiction. All three influence banking, visas, bookkeeping and ongoing administration. Treating each requirement as a separate transaction creates avoidable gaps.

We begin with the whole business, establish the appropriate sequence and coordinate the agreed work through one accountable Relationship Manager.

FLAGSHIP ENGAGEMENT

Complete UAE Establishment

For international groups that require more than a standard formation package. We assess the business, compare appropriate mainland and free-zone routes, provide a written commercial recommendation and coordinate the agreed work through formation and operational set-up.

Depending on the scope, this can include banking preparation, residence applications, bookkeeping set-up, corporate administration and tax-specialist coordination.
